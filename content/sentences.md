## Case 1 · Be Inspector（14 题）

- `b01` [O] She is a suspect, **isn't she**?
- `b02` [O] They are not thieves, **are they**?
- `b03` [O] The dinosaur egg is valuable, **isn't it**?
- `b04` [O] We aren't suspects, **are we**?
- `b05` [O] Tom is drawing hieroglyphics, **isn't he**?
- `b06` [O] Lucy is a university student, **isn't she**?
- `b07` [O] The museum windows are closed, **aren't they**?
- `b08` [O] You aren't angry with the police, **are you**?
- `b09` [O] The old museum is very beautiful, **isn't it**?
- `b10` [O] The policeman is here, **isn't he**?
- `b11` [O] He isn't good at football, **is he**?
- `b12` [WB] We're rich, **aren't we**?
- `b13` [WB] The treasure is under this tree, **isn't it**?
- `b14` [WB] There's a photo of it in the guide book, **isn't there**?
